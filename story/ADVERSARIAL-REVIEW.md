## Steelman counterarguments

### Against C2-H1 (independence)

Monogenesis-via-stimulus remains live for Egypt↔Mesopotamia. “Independently in
several parts” can be read as stronger than “at least some inventions were
independent.” **Mitigation already in prose** — keep the soften; do not upgrade
to “four pristine scripts, settled forever.”

### Against C2-H2 / practical origins

Even Mesopotamia’s “admin first” story is a Schmandt-Besserat-led reconstruction;
debates continue about tokens→writing. China/Egypt first genres undercut any
universal practical list — **already softened**.

### Against C2-H3 (external memory threshold)

If tokens already separate data from knower, the essay’s civilizational “turn”
may be overstated. **Mitigation:** threshold language (“more powerfully than
earlier counters”) — keep; do not reintroduce “for the first time.”

### Against C5-O1 / O2

Bus-factor metrics from VCS overstate code ownership vs tacit context; Rigby et
al. show simplistic truck factors exaggerate disaster. Some firms survive key
departures. **Mitigation:** essay already says “often”; research pack notes
mitigation via successors. Do not claim inevitability.

### Against C2-P2 (limit → tool)

Teleology risk: we narrate successes backward. Empires used khipu/oral systems
without “writing”; some limits produce collapse or inequality of access, not
tools. **Mitigation:** historical parallel bounds the claim as _functional
externalization_, not writing-only progress.

### Against C7-I3 (destinations ≫ paths)

Talmudic commentary and ADRs are institutionalized path preservation. Claiming
civilization is “comparatively less skilled” at paths ignores those crafts.
**Mitigation:** soft prose to comparative scarcity amid real exceptions
(applied in Ch. 7).

### Against the thesis spine (civilization as accumulation of understanding)

Tech-determinist steelman: tools _are_ the cause, not evidence of deeper
coordination. Inequality steelman: “accumulation” was often extraction; many
communities were excluded from the archive. **Not resolved here** — flag for
later chapters / adversarial human review; do not paper over in Ch. 1–8 softens.
