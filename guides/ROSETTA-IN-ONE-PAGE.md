# Rosetta in one page

**Status:** Sketch
**Altitude:** The Story — *Why should this exist?*
**Audience:** educated general reader

This is a one-page restatement of the essay's destination, not a replacement
for it. Rosetta is named here because this page is an explanation, not the
manuscript. The long-form argument still withholds the name until
[Chapter 10](../story/chapter-10.md).

---

Civilization became extraordinarily good at preserving **what we
concluded**.

It remains surprisingly bad at preserving **how we came to conclude it**.

A tax bill arrives. The number survived. The path usually did not: the
rates, the budgets, the hearings, the staff recommendations, the
conditions those recommendations were answering. The destination is easy
to keep. The route that made it intelligible is not.

The same pattern shows up in smaller rooms. A team inherits a system whose
purpose is still documented and whose evolution is not. A person can
remember a belief and lose the conversations that produced it. A
generation can leave behind laws, tools, and institutions while leaving
the next one to guess why those things took their present form.

We do not merely need more memory.

We need enough of the relationships among **experience, evidence,
interpretation, decision, and consequence** that a later mind can
reconstruct the journey honestly — including the honesty of saying when
part of the path is missing.

```text
PERSON
"What happened to me?"

        ↓

TEAM / ORGANIZATION
"Why did we do this?"

        ↓

COMMUNITY / GOVERNMENT
"Why was this decided?"

        ↓

CIVILIZATION
"How did we get here?"
```

Same question. Different scale.

**Chronicle** keeps enough of the path to investigate it.
**Wayfinder** lets a person ask.
**Rosetta** is the practice and infrastructure that make that exchange
possible.

If Rosetta disappeared tomorrow, the need would remain. The measure is not
whether a particular name is remembered. The measure is whether later
travelers inherit a conversation they can continue, rather than a pile of
conclusions they must take on faith.
