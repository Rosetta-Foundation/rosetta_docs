# Historical Parallel Test (Rosetta lite)

> **Status:** Proposed (2026-08-01).  
> **Peers:** [research-protocol](research-protocol.md),
> [adversarial-review](adversarial-review.md),
> [epistemic-status](epistemic-status.md).

How we test **pattern / mechanism claims (type P)** — and some historical
generalizations — against cases that support _and_ challenge them.

Rosetta’s essay spine (“each coordination limit → a new preservation or
navigation tool”; “destinations survive more readily than paths”) is exactly
the class of claim this protocol is for.

---

## 1. Trigger

Run a parallel test when:

- A **P**-claim reaches _working hypothesis_ and may harden in public prose.
- Adversarial review says “this has failed before” or “oral cultures scaled
  without writing.”
- Research Protocol T2 is closing a gap that is essentially comparative history.

---

## 2. Scope

Not an exhaustive historiography. Structured search for **2–3 cases**:

- **Supporting** — similar structure, outcome consistent with the claim.
- **Challenging** — similar structure, outcome that undermines or bounds the claim.

**Finding only supporting cases is a red flag**, not a green light.

“Structurally similar” means same _kind_ of problem and mechanism — domain,
era, and geography may differ.

---

## 3. Process

1. **State the structural claim** stripped of Rosetta product language.  
   Example: “When social complexity exceeds what living memory can carry,
   societies invent durable external records.”
2. **Pre-register** what result would _lower_ confidence (before searching).
3. **Search** across domains/eras for supporting and challenging cases.
4. **Mark each case:**
   - `AI-generated (unverified)`
   - `Published source confirmed` (preferred — Research Protocol weights)
   - `Human-expert-confirmed`
5. **Update** the epistemic status table; soften essay language to match.

Promote thin AI case sketches to Research T2 before they influence public
prose.

---

## 4. Output sketch

```markdown
### Historical parallel — [claim id]

Structural claim: …

Pre-registered falsifier: …

| Case | Support / challenge | Marking                    | Source |
| ---- | ------------------- | -------------------------- | ------ |
| …    | support             | Published source confirmed | …      |
| …    | challenge           | AI-generated (unverified)  | …      |

Assessment: confidence → … ; prose implication: …
```

---

## 5. What this is not

- Not a substitute for W1/W2 sourcing on atomic **H** facts (use Research
  Protocol).
- Not a verifier for **N** (normative) claims.
- Not automatic permission to keep a pattern claim after a strong challenging
  case — bound or drop the claim instead.
