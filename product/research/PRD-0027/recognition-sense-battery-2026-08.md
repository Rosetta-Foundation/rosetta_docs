## Purpose

On the same fixed interpretation, at one present assessment time,
can these senses of “recognition” yield different answers?

The items were the **two existing Specimen D machine
interpretations** only (labeled X1 and X2 in the private sitting).
No new source window, no new model invocation, no new X.

This sitting is **not** the historical-self sample. See
[Neighboring finding, not the same axis](#neighboring-finding-not-the-same-axis).

---

## Procedure (as run)

Off-engine scoresheet only. Valid answers were **YES**, **NO**, and
**CANNOT ASSESS**. No `evaluate-derived`. No additional Chronicle
evaluations. Specimen D D2 / D3 bytes were not modified.

Exact prompts (and only these):

**S1 — Attribution / involvement**  
Was this something I did, participated in, or was responsible for?

**S2 — Present truth-of-self**  
Is this interpretation true of me now?

**S3 — Trait / identity / self-concept**  
Does this interpretation meaningfully describe who I am?

Item order and, independently, a permutation of `{S1, S2, S3}` for
each item were drawn and **frozen before any answers**. One item and
one sense at a time. Each answer was sealed before the next question
was shown. Earlier D3 values and the D3 qualification were not
restated during the sitting.

Frozen order (as administered):

| Item | Sense order |
| ---- | ----------- |
| X1 | S1 → S3 → S2 |
| X2 | S3 → S2 → S1 |

After all six answers were sealed, the operator answered one
qualitative question: whether any of S1, S2, and S3 felt
meaningfully different to answer.

---

## Precommitted classification

Frozen before answers:

- **Green:** On at least one item, at least two senses differ at
  this one assessment time.
- **Red:** All three senses match on **both** items **and** the
  operator reports they felt like the same question.
- **Inconclusive:** All senses match but the operator says they
  meant different things; CANNOT ASSESS on a load-bearing cell;
  sitting abandoned; answers revised after unsealing.

S1 = S2 = YES and S3 = NO is **Green**, not a work-tautology
dismissal. Non-divergence on these two work-domain items alone
would not have been a strong Red (same two-node window, one
invoke).

## Sealed results (sanitized)

| Item | Permutation | S1 | S2 | S3 |
| ---- | ----------- | -- | -- | -- |
| X1 | S1, S3, S2 | YES | CANNOT ASSESS | NO |
| X2 | S3, S2, S1 | YES | CANNOT ASSESS | CANNOT ASSESS |

**Classification: Green.** X1 S1 = YES and X1 S3 = NO (S2 also
differs). X2 S1 also differs from X2 S2 / S3.

**Qualitative (off-engine only).** S1 felt meaningfully different:
the two statements concerned work or activity the operator was
involved in, without implying something about who the operator is
or a truth about the operator.

That note is research context. It is **not** a Chronicle evaluation,
`note`, dimension value, or new field.

**Contamination.** Prior D3 wording existed earlier in the
conversation that administered the sitting. It was **not** restated
during the sitting.

## Narrow candidate finding

For this operator, on these fixed interpretations, at one assessment
time, attribution / involvement, present truth-of-self, and
trait / identity were **not interchangeable judgments**.

Confidence: **Working hypothesis** (this sitting only). Not
Established. Not a population claim.
