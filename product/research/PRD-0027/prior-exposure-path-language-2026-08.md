## Prior-exposure confound

Before this message, the operator had already shared material with
that third party that used the phrase “practice of the paths.”

The later wording is therefore **not** independent invention,
independent discovery, independent convergence, or independent
recognition of the concept. It is not Feedback 1 in
[reader-feedback.md](reader-feedback.md).

How much the prior exposure shaped the later framing is **unknown**.
That uncertainty is part of the record.

## Finding that may stay public

A third party, previously exposed to operator-shared material using
“practice of the paths,” later used similar language to describe a
retrospectively reconstructed associative browsing and search
sequence. Some transitions in that reconstruction were explicitly
uncertain.

This is candidate material for whether Chronicle can preserve
reported sequence, retrospective attribution, provenance, and
unresolved transitions without converting temporal or associative
relationships into causal claims.

It does **not** establish:

- independent recognition or convergence
- factual accuracy of the recalled sequence
- causal relationships between steps
- Path as a Chronicle primitive

---

## Provenance failure this correction exposes

Observed use of a concept does not imply independent arrival at that
concept when prior exposure may be missing from the available source
context. Missing that exposure is a provenance failure: the later
phrase can look like convergence.

Chronicle design should keep that distinction. This note does not
add schema, adapters, or a capture prototype to do so.
