# Reader feedback

Preserved as research input. Do not treat these notes as a mandate to expand
[PRD-0027](../../PRD-0027-personal-chronicle.md). Paraphrase and interpretation
belong in the brief; this file keeps the received wording.

The original reader messages were not attached to the research handoff. The
verbatim record below is the operator-curated summary used to open this phase.
If the source messages become available, append them here without rewriting
these entries.

---

## Feedback 1: Independent recognition of the Path concept

A reader independently responded to the Rosetta story with an observation very
close to the emerging core hypothesis:

> “The path between ideas feels like a critical factor of memory and knowledge
> transfer.”

They further suggested that the structure of curation may sometimes be more
significant than the individual facts, figures, or data. They compared this to
geometry: individual points matter, but relationships between points create a
structure containing additional meaning.

### Interpretation (research, not product change)

This is early evidence that the Path concept is independently legible. The
reader was not given a formal theory of Paths and nevertheless identified
relationships, sequencing, and curation as carriers of meaning.

That does not prove the hypothesis. It does suggest that Rosetta may be naming
something people already experience but lack a good primitive for describing.

---

## Feedback 2: “What is this category?”

Other readers may understand the individual components—notes, memory, context,
history, learning, reflection—while still having trouble placing the whole
thing in a familiar product category.

### Interpretation (research, not product change)

This may be category confusion rather than conceptual failure.

A new primitive is often hard to describe through existing categories because
those categories divide the problem differently. A Personal Chronicle is not
merely a journal, a second brain, a CRM, a knowledge graph, a feed, a
timeline, or an AI memory store. It may draw from each without reducing to any
of them.

Do not treat every request for familiar framing as evidence that the idea
should be forced back into an existing box. Clarify enough to make the work
usable; preserve the possibility that the box is the wrong unit of analysis.

---

## Feedback 3: Concerns about surveillance, over-collection, and AI authority

Readers are rightly alert to systems that infer too much, retain too much, or
become difficult to correct.

### Interpretation (research, not product change)

This is not peripheral product feedback. It is central design material. If
Rosetta represents a person’s path, the person must retain meaningful control
over what is captured, how it is interpreted, what is visible, what is
retained, and what is forgotten.

---

## Handling rules

1. New reader comments append below this line, dated, with attribution if the
   reader consents.
2. Do not silently “clean up” disagreement.
3. Category-confusion questions feed the [language test](language-test.md) and
   [landscape](landscape.md), not PRD scope.
4. Surveillance and authority concerns feed
   [privacy-and-forgetting.md](privacy-and-forgetting.md) before any capture
   prototype.
